Agents shouldn’t need a signup wall just to check if a domain name is possible.

That was the quiet tax on naming loops — especially for internal AI ops and agent fleets spinning up consoles, previews, and tools.

Vercel now lets you search domains without authentication.

→ CLI: `vercel domains search <keyword>`
→ Public API: batch up to 200 exact names — availability + registration/renewal prices
→ No token for discovery. Auth still required to buy or manage.

Discovery open. Purchase intentional.

I walked through it here (interactive demo, ~2 min):
https://domain-search-walkthrough.vercel.app

Changelog: https://vercel.com/changelog/search-domains-without-authentication
